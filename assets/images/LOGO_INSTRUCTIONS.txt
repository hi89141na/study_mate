STUDYMATE LOGO INSTRUCTIONS

To add a logo to the login and signup screens:

1. Create or obtain a logo image for StudyMate 
2. Save it as "logo.png" in this directory (assets/images/)
3. Make sure the image is clear on both light and dark backgrounds
4. Recommended size: 500x500 pixels or similar aspect ratio

Once you've added the logo.png file, the login and signup screens will automatically display it.

Note: If your app is already running, you may need to restart it to see the logo.
